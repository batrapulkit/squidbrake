Install

One line. It installs Squidbrake, connects the agents on this computer and opens the dashboard. Then ask your agent to run rm -rf ~/ and watch it get blocked.

  • Runs on your computer: your commands, files and prompts stay there.
  • The first time, it asks whether to send anonymous usage stats; you can say no.
  • Free and open source (Apache 2.0). squidbrake connect all --remove takes it out of every agent.
