What every plan includes

Rules that allow, hold or block each action an agent takes; approvals from the dashboard, your phone, Slack, Teams or email, with Face ID or fingerprint if you want them signed; a stop button for one agent, one conversation or all of them; an undo copy before risky changes; a hash-chained audit trail with signed checkpoints, exported as CSV or JSON and checkable by anyone; evidence packs mapped to SOC 2, ISO/IEC 42001 and the EU AI Act; spend limits per agent; checks that what an agent says it did really happened; and, with your own Anthropic key, a short AI brief on each held action.

Questions

Is anything held back from the free version?
No. The paid plans are hosting, setup and support. If you self-host, you have the same software we run.
Where does my data live on a hosted plan?
On a server we operate. If it has to stay inside your network, self-host it, or take Enterprise and we'll set it up in your cloud. See Security for exactly what's stored.
Do you have SOC 2 or ISO 27001?
Not yet. We hold no certifications today. Squidbrake produces evidence for your audits; it doesn't make us certified.
Who pays for the AI briefs?
You do, directly: they use your own Anthropic key, and are off until you add one.
What's not built yet?
Single sign-on, keeping data in a chosen region, uptime guarantees, and a console for service providers managing many client companies. They're listed so you don't have to ask.
