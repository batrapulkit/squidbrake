    Questions

    What counts as an action?
    One tool call, command or API call that an agent sends through Squidbrake for a decision. Opening the dashboard or approving something doesn't count.
    What happens when I run out of actions?
    On Free, recording stops until next month. On paid plans, more actions are added automatically and your rules keep working. You can turn this off or set a monthly cap.
    Is anything held back from the open-source version?
    Blocking and holding never are: self-hosted, they're free. Approvals on a phone, Slack, Teams or email, the audit export, more than one person and SSO need a license, by the plans above. On our hosted plans, Free only watches; allowing, approving and denying start on Starter.
    Is there an offer for early teams?
    Yes. The first 10 teams get Team for $99 a month for a year. Email us to claim it.
    Where does my data live on a hosted plan?
    On a server we operate. If it has to stay inside your network, self-host it, or take Enterprise and we'll set it up in your cloud. See Security for exactly what's stored.
    Do you have SOC 2 or ISO 27001?
    Not yet. We hold no certifications today. Squidbrake produces evidence for your audits; it doesn't make us certified.
    Who pays for the AI briefs?
    You do, directly: they use your own Anthropic key, and are off until you add one.
    What's on the roadmap?
    Blast radius for each agent, SCIM, keeping data in a chosen region, an uptime SLA, and a console for service providers managing many client companies. None is built yet; they're listed so you don't have to ask.
